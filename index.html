    Anchors

    • Eq. Pacific band ~±10°
    • Ascent west warm pool (~130°E class)
    • Descent east cold tongue (~90°W class)
    • Lower easterlies / upper westerlies
    • El Niño: weaker, ascent east
    • La Niña: stronger, steeper tilt

    How

    Pacific Walker: equatorial zonal overturning — ascent over the Maritime Continent/warm pool (~120–150°E), descent over the eastern Pacific (~80–100°W); lower easterlies, upper westerlies. Particles are a ±5–10° toy, not Hadley or storm tracks.
